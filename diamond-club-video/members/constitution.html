The Constitution of Diamond Club 99 of Ekiti

Enacted on 25 November 1999, reviewed in 2013 and amended in 2023, the constitution sets out the Club's vision, membership rules, financial obligations, leadership structure and meeting procedures. Every member is entitled to the full text.

FOUNDED25 November 1999
REVIEWED2013
AMENDED2023
MOTTOUnited We Stand

1Vision & Mission

Vision: To provide quality leadership towards the cultural, economic, social and political development of Ekitiland and her people.

Mission: The Club shall promote and uphold the highest standards of our values, culture, economic development, political aspirations and social well-being of our Members and Ekiti people.

2Membership & Admission

  • Open to eligible Ekiti men of unquestionable character with a reasonable economic, educational and social background.
  • Intending members apply for admission and obtain an application form for ₦10,000, non-refundable.
  • Completed applications are referred to Executive Members of the Club.
  • Admitted members are issued a letter of acceptance and must submit a letter of acceptance of offer within one month.
  • Upon satisfaction of admission requirements, members are inducted and issued a membership number.

3Financial Obligations

  • Admission fee: ₦10,000, paid with the letter of acceptance of admission.
  • Annual subscription: ₦10,000.
  • Other levies may be imposed by the General House as needs arise.
  • Members aged 70 and above have the option of paying half of their dues.
  • A member loses standing if financial obligations remain unmet for 3 continuous months, and risks loss of membership after a further 3 months from a written default notice.

4Meetings

  • The Monthly General Meeting holds on the last Sunday of each month at 3:00 PM, at a venue approved by the General House.
  • The Executive Committee meets monthly at a date and venue approved by the Committee or President.
  • Emergency meetings may be summoned as provided in the constitution.

5Organs of the Club

  • The General House
  • The Executive Committee
  • The Board of Trustees and Agba Egbe
  • Any other standing or Ad Hoc Committee approved by the General House

6Executive Officers & Terms

Elected officers serve a two-year term, with a maximum of two continuous terms, except where noted.
