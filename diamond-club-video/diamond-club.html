Who we are

About Diamond Club 99 of Ekiti

The constitution states that Ekiti elite came together on 25 November 1999 to form Diamond Club 99 of Ekiti, with the aim of harmonizing opinions on important national issues, promoting the welfare of Ekiti people, and advancing the progress and unity of Ekiti land.

Brotherhood

Genuine friendships built on trust, accountability, and shared purpose.

Mentorship

Encouraging influential members and Ekiti indigenes to mentor younger Ekiti sons and daughters.

Ekiti Progress

Promoting the cultural, economic, political and social progress of Ekiti people.

Consultation & Dialogue

Using consultation and dialogue to harmonize divergent opinions affecting Ekiti land.

The constitution also provides for cooperation, friendship and understanding within the Club and with other Ekiti organizations, while encouraging members to pursue sustainable personal economic goals and support younger people to achieve higher social and economic goals.

The Club's governing framework

Built Around the Constitution

The Constitution of Diamond Club 99 of Ekiti sets out the Club's identity, vision, mission, membership rules, financial obligations, organs, leadership responsibilities, meetings, trusteeship, banking, auditing and dissolution procedures.

MOTTO UNITED WE STAND
VISIONTo provide quality leadership toward the culture, economic, social and political development of Ekitiland and her people.
MembershipOpen to eligible Ekiti men of unquestionable character with reasonable economic, educational and social background.
MISSIONThe club shall promot and uphold the highest standard of our values, culture, economic development, political aspirations and social well-being of our members and Ekiti people
General MeetingMonthly general meeting: last Sunday of each month at 3 pm, at a venue approved by the General House.
AIMS AND OBJECTIVESi. To cater for the interest and welfare of its members. ii. To promot the cultural, economic, political and social progress of Ekiti people as well as unity and progress of Ekiti land. iii. To encourage, promote and foster co-operation, friendship and understand within the club and other Ekiti organizations.
LeadershipPresident and Vice President serve two-year terms, with a maximum of two continuous terms.
OrgansGeneral House, Executive Committee, Board of Trustees and Agba Egbe, plus approved standing or ad hoc committees.

Membership & Admission

Intending members apply through an application form, which the constitution states costs N10,000 non-refundable. Completed applications are referred to Executive Members, followed by the Club's admission and induction process.
